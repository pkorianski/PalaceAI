## Description

Your table. Your journey.

Play Palace, the classic card-shedding game, with a quick hand whenever you want—or take your seat on the Palace Tour.

QUICK PLAY
Jump into a solo game against a computer opponent. Build your palace, match or beat the pile, and keep a 2 or 10 ready when you need a little magic.

A TOUR WORTH RETURNING TO
Travel through four venues and meet sixteen rivals with different play styles. Earn Bronze, Silver and Gold titles, challenge venue champions, and collect trophies as you master each table.

MAKE THE TABLE YOURS
Unlock card and table themes through your Tour. Your earned trophies stay yours, even when a later match doesn’t go your way.

A NEW WEEK, A NEW RUN
Unlock the weekly solo circuit and work toward its ribbons against a rotating lineup of computer opponents. The circuit resets each Monday at 00:00 UTC.

BUILD YOUR LEGACY
Earn mastery XP with every finished game, chase long-term milestones, and equip earned player titles and table styles. Rotating weekly quests reward steady play with permanent dated seals. Your lifetime progress stays, even when you take a break.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
A visual guide and complete rules help you learn. Settings let you control haptics and reduce motion. The installed app plays offline without an account, ads or in-app purchases.

Tour progress is saved locally on your device. Quick Play starts a fresh deal when reopened. Cloud sync and multiplayer are not included.

## Reviewer notes

Palace is a single-player offline card game. It requires no login or development server in the installed production build. Quick Play is available immediately. Palace Tour is a separate persistent career; later venues, title tiers, themes and the weekly solo circuit unlock through play. All opponents are local computer-controlled players. There are no payments, ads, real-money wagering or online chat.

For the rules or visual guide: home → Settings → How to Play / Learn Palace. Privacy, Support and version/build information are in Settings. Reset game progress requires confirmation and permanently clears local game data while preserving preferences.

The game has a short, skippable Kori Games ident after launch. Device Reduce Motion is respected. No special permissions or demo credentials are required.
