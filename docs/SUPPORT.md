# Palace support

Need a hand? Contact **Korianski Games** at [pkorianski.engr@gmail.com](mailto:pkorianski.engr@gmail.com?subject=Palace%20support).

Questions, feedback and bug reports are welcome. Include your app version (in Settings), device model, what happened and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots help. Never send passwords or sensitive information.

## Where is my progress saved?

Your Tour, trophies, statistics, achievements and preferences are stored on your device. Tour resumes your saved deal. Quick Play starts a fresh deal when reopened. Cloud sync and cross-device restore are not available. Deleting the app or using Reset game progress removes local progress; we cannot recover it. Your device backups are managed separately through Apple or your device provider.

## Can I play offline?

The installed App Store/TestFlight app plays offline. Opening websites and sending support email need an internet connection. Expo Go during development needs the development server.

## Are these online opponents?

Quick Play, Tour rivals and the weekly circuit use local computer opponents. The weekly circuit refreshes on Mondays at 00:00 UTC. Multiplayer is not included.

## How do I change feedback or animation?

Open Settings from the home screen. Turn Haptics off or enable Reduce motion. The device’s Reduce Motion setting is always respected. You can also replay the visual guide or read the full rules.

## Privacy and deletion

Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md). Reset game progress erases your local game history and saved Tour while preserving your preferences. For deletion of support correspondence, email the address above.
