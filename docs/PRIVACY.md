# Palace privacy policy

Updated November 18, 2026

## Your game stays on your device

Palace is an offline, single-player card game by Korianski Games. Your Tour progress, trophies, statistics, starter badges, mastery XP, dated activity, weekly quest progress and seals, equipped titles/table styles, daily challenge progress, tutorial status and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a gameplay server or send your gameplay or usage analytics to us.

## No accounts, ads or tracking

You do not need an account to play. Palace contains no advertising or tracking SDKs. The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo library. Opponents are local computer-controlled players.

## Your choices and deletion

In Settings, you can control haptics and reduce motion, and use Reset game progress to erase your local Tour, trophies, statistics, starter badges, mastery XP, weekly quests and seals, equipped rewards, challenges and tutorial history. Your preferences remain. Deleting the app also removes its local data; copies included in device backups are managed through your Apple or device backup settings. We cannot recover local progress or transfer it between devices.

## Support and external websites

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and message. We use it to respond and resolve your request, and retain it only as long as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. Do not send passwords or sensitive information. You can email us to request deletion of support correspondence. Opening our public support/privacy pages uses GitHub, which may process standard access logs under GitHub’s privacy policy. Your email provider and Apple’s App Store/TestFlight services have their own privacy practices.

## Children and policy changes

Palace does not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. Please contact us about any privacy concern. If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and any required App Store disclosures before releasing the change.
