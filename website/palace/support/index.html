Where is my progress saved?

Your Tour, trophies, starter badges, mastery XP, weekly quests and seals, statistics and preferences are stored locally on your device. Tour can resume your saved deal. Quick Play starts a fresh deal when reopened. Cloud sync and cross-device restore are not available. Deleting the app or choosing Reset game progress removes local progress; we cannot recover it.

Can I play offline?

The installed App Store/TestFlight app can play offline. Support email and external websites need an internet connection. Expo Go used during development also needs the development server.

Are these online opponents?

No. Quick Play, Tour rivals and the weekly circuit all use local computer opponents. The weekly circuit refreshes on Mondays at 00:00 UTC. Multiplayer is not included.

How do I turn off vibrations or animation?

Open Settings from the home screen. Turn Haptics off, or enable Reduce motion. Your device’s Reduce Motion setting is always respected.
